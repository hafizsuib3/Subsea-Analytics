## 📊 Project Overview

Subsea Analytics is an end-to-end Business Intelligence portfolio project designed to demonstrate the transformation of raw business information into meaningful analytical insights.

The project focuses on integrating operational records, procurement reports, ERP exports, and manually maintained Excel datasets into a structured reporting environment.

Data is extracted, validated, standardized, and transformed before being stored in Microsoft SQL Server. SQL views and structured data models are subsequently used to support Power BI reporting and analytical calculations.

The resulting dashboards provide interactive visualizations, performance indicators, and analytical perspectives to support business monitoring and management reporting.

### Project Objectives

- Demonstrate end-to-end Business Intelligence development.
- Integrate and transform data from multiple business sources.
- Develop structured data models for analytical reporting.
- Implement dynamic DAX measures and performance indicators.
- Design interactive and user-friendly Power BI dashboards.
- Improve reporting accessibility and analytical visibility.
- Continuously enhance dashboard functionality and performance.
